## How it works
**Nobody has to run a process.**

**1. Connect the tools your teams already use.**
Same day, no engineering. Nobody tags, logs or forwards anything.

**2. It works out what matters.**
A passing "that would be nice" doesn't count the same as a customer who built a workaround. Every signal is tied to the account it came from and, through your CRM, the revenue behind it. When a customer says they're evaluating alternatives, that conversation is flagged at risk. Recurring problems and strong frustration are flagged to watch. You see it the week it's said, with the call it came from, not at the renewal meeting.

**3. It reaches you where you work.**
Each team gets reports on its own area, daily, weekly or monthly, and you can follow your tier 1 accounts and churn signals. Keep planning in Jira or Linear, and ProductSense pushes plans there. It can take over the feedback side of a PM tool you've stopped maintaining.

## One idea, researched
**Eyebrow:** Agentic product discovery
**Bring an idea. Get the case for and against.**
Agents do the research. Your team decides.

Start from your own idea, or from one that surfaced in customer conversations. ProductSense finds the evidence for it, the evidence against it, and the caveats:

| Illustrative example: audit log export | |
|---|---|
| **Who wants it** | 14 accounts, €1.2M in annual revenue |
| **What supports it** | 41 conversations in six months, 9 described workarounds |
| **What argues against it** | 2 large accounts asked for the opposite. Self-serve customers never mention it. |
| **Caveats** | Most requests come from EU accounts. Partners haven't mentioned it either way. |

_Illustrative numbers. In ProductSense, every number traces back to the conversations behind it, so checking doesn't take an afternoon._

Support is easy to find for any idea. What helps you decide is knowing who doesn't need it, and where the evidence is thin.

## FAQ
**Is ProductSense a fit for my company?** _(rewritten)_
ProductSense is built for B2B software companies where many people talk to customers every week and the product teams can't keep up with all of it. Usually that means several product teams, and key accounts that matter to revenue. If one product person is still in every customer call, you probably don't need us yet.

**What is agentic product discovery?** _(new)_
Product discovery is finding out what's worth building. Usually that means scheduling interviews and reading through notes by hand. In agentic product discovery, agents do the research work. They go through the conversations your company has already had, map them to your product, accounts and revenue, and find the evidence for and against the ideas you bring. People still decide what to build.

**Do we need to use Claude or Cursor?** _(new)_
No. You can use ProductSense entirely in the web app and through scheduled reports. Claude and Cursor are for the people who already work there and want customer evidence in the same place.
